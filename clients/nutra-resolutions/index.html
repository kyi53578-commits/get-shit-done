Process

Six steps, start to shipment.

We provide comprehensive turnkey manufacturing services — from formulation and sourcing to production and packaging — helping position your business for growth and long-term success.

Step 01

Concept Review

We review your product goals, target format, and volume, and confirm what’s feasible before anyone spends money on it.

Step 02

Formulation

Your concept becomes a working formula — actives, excipients, and dosing specified against the claims you want on the label.

Step 03

Sourcing

Raw materials are sourced to the formula specification and verified against their documentation before they enter production.

Step 04

Production Run

Your batch is manufactured under cGMP-compliant procedures, with in-process checks recorded as the run proceeds.

Step 05

Quality & Compliance

Finished product is checked against specification and the batch record is completed, giving you traceable documentation for the run.

Step 06

Packaging & Fulfillment

Filled, labelled, and packed to your packaging specification — full design, label-only, or shipped in bulk for private label finishing.
